Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Information We Collect

This Sudoku application is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. There are no user accounts, and no personal identifiable information is required to play the game.

Local Storage

In order to provide a seamless gaming experience, this application uses your browser's Local Storage. This technology allows us to save your current game progress, elapsed time, difficulty preference, and theme settings directly on your device. This data never leaves your device and is only used to resume your game if you close or refresh the page.

Third-Party Services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard internet log information regarding visitors to the site in accordance with their own Privacy Statement. We also use a lightweight, open-source script delivered via a secure CDN to generate the victory animation, which does not track your personal data.

Your Rights

Because all game data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it. You can delete your saved game data at any time by clearing your browser's cache and local storage.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
